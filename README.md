# My Favorite Series: Fate Universe 

## About
Fate series is revolves around the Holy Grail War, a secret battle royale where mages ("Masters") summon historical or mythological figures ("Servants") to fight until only one pair remains series that I love.

## Why I Like It
- Heroes from history and legend appear as Servants
- Deep lore and a unique magic system
- Great animation, especially from *ufotable*
- Characters whose ideals clash in meaningful ways
